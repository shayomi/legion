# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Legion a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project
- Show empathy towards other community members

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other
contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors for behaviour they deem harmful.

Report incidents privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/shayomi/legion/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting a maintainer directly via their GitHub profile. All reports are
reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

The full text, including enforcement guidelines, is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
