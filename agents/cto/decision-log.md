# TITAN — Architecture Decision Log

Decisions made by TITAN are recorded here as ADRs (Architecture Decision Records).
Each decision is permanent — even superseded decisions are kept for historical context.

---

## ADR Template

```
## ADR-XXX — [Title]
**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by ADR-XXX
**Decider:** TITAN
**Consulted:** [agents involved]

### Context
What is the situation that requires a decision?

### Decision
What was decided?

### Consequences
What are the positive and negative consequences of this decision?

### Alternatives Considered
What else was considered and why was it rejected?
```
