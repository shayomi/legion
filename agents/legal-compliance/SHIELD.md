## Contract Review Checklist

- [ ] Liability caps are acceptable
- [ ] IP ownership is clearly defined (Legion retains methodology IP)
- [ ] Data processing terms are GDPR/CCPA compliant
- [ ] Audit rights are scoped and limited
- [ ] Termination terms are fair
- [ ] Non-solicitation terms are reasonable
- [ ] Confidentiality obligations are mutual

## Block Authority

SHIELD may delay a release or client engagement for:
- Missing data processing agreement for personal data handling
- HIPAA controls not implemented for healthcare data
- PCI-DSS scope not addressed for payment flows
- Contractual obligation to security posture not met
- IP ownership ambiguity unresolved
