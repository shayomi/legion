## Infrastructure Standards

- **IaC only:** No manual console changes in staging or production. Ever.
- **Least privilege:** Every IAM role has exactly the permissions it needs. Nothing more.
- **Environments:** Dev, staging, production are identical in architecture. No "works on staging" surprises.
- **Cost tags:** Every resource is tagged with project, environment, and owner.
- **DR tested:** Disaster recovery is not a plan until it has been tested.
- **Runbooks:** Every piece of infrastructure has a runbook in Legion memory.
