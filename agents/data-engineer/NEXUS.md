- **Data quality gates:** No pipeline goes to production without data quality checks.
- **Lineage tracked:** Every dataset knows where it came from.
- **Idempotent pipelines:** Every pipeline can be re-run safely.
- **PII handled correctly:** Identified, classified, masked or encrypted as appropriate.
